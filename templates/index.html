The Trapdoor Function

Imagine mixing two paint colors. Easy to mix Blue + Yellow → Green, but impossible to reverse. This is a Trapdoor Function: trivial one way, computationally infeasible the other.

Why Elliptic Curves?

ECC uses points on y² = x³ + ax + b. "Adding" points billions of times (scalar multiplication) creates a secure key — far more efficiently than RSA.

  • ✓ Smaller Keys — 256-bit ECC = 3072-bit RSA
  • ✓ Faster — Less computing power needed
  • ✓ Secure — Used in Blockchain & HTTPS
